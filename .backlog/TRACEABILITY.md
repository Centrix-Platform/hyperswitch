## Review corrections incorporated

- Secure credential provision precedes MCA materialization; core authorization is independent of portal OIDC.
- Opaque-key pre-auth lookup and RLS-compatible job discovery have narrowly scoped interfaces.
- Native automatic/manual/clear-PAN retry expansion is constrained and exercised in financial fault gates.
- The CTX-013-selected terminal branch is sufficient for the first integration; both adapters are not a conjunction.
- JWE and vendor-owned authorization activate explicit conditional implementation/security/certification dependencies.
- Headless SDK delivery is an explicit accepted branding/resource outcome, not a requirement to add optional UI.
- Disputes/chargebacks include backend attribution and portal/evidence/manual-hand-off responsibilities.
- Domain branding, platform console and upstream maintenance are included in qualification/launch closure.
- CTX-107 is sandbox/certification qualification; CTX-136 owns the independently approved limited live pilot and CTX-137 broad launch.
- Deferred Kotlin and ledger programs are excluded from the initial direct-settlement launch requirements.
